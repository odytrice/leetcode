# Drill 198: House Robber

LeetCode Medium: https://leetcode.com/problems/house-robber/

Choose nonadjacent houses to maximize the sum of money robbed. Return that maximum. Houses form a line, not a circle.

Constraints: 1 to 100 nonnegative values, each at most 400.

Example: `[2,7,9,3,1]` returns `12`. Implement `Rob(int[] nums)`.
